Professional Summary – Michael Ghai Tong Aroffe

I am a South Sudanese media professional, peace advocate, and community leader with a strong background in journalism, communication, and youth empowerment. With over a decade of hands-on experience in radio broadcasting, public service, and grassroots activism, I specialize in using media as a tool for social change, peacebuilding, and amplifying marginalized voices.

Beginning as a volunteer teacher and later serving in senior roles such as Program Manager at Radio Duk Padiet 96.6 FM and Radio Jonglei 95.9 FM, I’ve led impactful storytelling initiatives that strengthen civic dialogue and community resilience. I also served as a Radio Inspector in the State Ministry of Information and held the role of Private Secretary to the Minister of Information in Jonglei State—gaining deep experience in media regulation and policy support.

My leadership journey includes serving as Chairperson of the Hol Youth Association, Secretary General of the Nyiel Community Association, and Information Secretary of the Jonglei Civil Society Network. I was honored to be selected as a Peace Fellow with the United Nations Mission in South Sudan (UNMISS) in 2021—an experience that broadened my global perspective and deepened my commitment to peace and development.

Achievements:

Led programming and editorial strategy for regional radio stations reaching thousands.

Co-founded the Duk Students Association to support under-resourced learners.

Advocated for youth rights and community development through various leadership roles.

Worked with civil society networks to promote accountability, justice, and human rights.


Looking for: I am actively seeking opportunities in media development, peacebuilding, international advocacy, or civil society engagement—particularly with organizations focused on community empowerment, human rights, or public communication.
